FAQ

Print shop questions

Do I still send proofs?

Yes, but they go through the platform. The buyer approves the proof there, and the job only moves to production after approval.

Can customers design products themselves?

Yes. The product designer is on Starter, Wholesale and Scale.

Where do I see what to print next?

In the production queue. Every job shows with its status, so the next one is clear.

Can I sell DTF gang sheets too?

Yes. The gang sheet builder is on the same plans and its jobs land in the same queue.

How does the commission work?

It applies to the printing charge on orders built in the gang sheet builder or the designer, at 2.8% on Starter, 1.9% on Wholesale and 1.3% on Scale. Blanks, shipping and tax are not included.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Your store runs under your brand and your domain.
