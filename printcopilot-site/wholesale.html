FAQ

Wholesale questions

Do my buyers need to call or email to order?

No. They order on your store, by grid or with Quick Buy, at their own price.

Can buyers have different prices?

Yes. Place buyers in customer groups and set tiered pricing for each group.

Do I approve who can buy?

Yes. Wholesale applications are reviewed and approved by you.

Can I offer net terms?

Yes. Net terms and credit are available at checkout, with invoices for those accounts.

Can I sell blanks?

Yes. Bring in supplier catalogs or load your own products by CSV, and keep stock in sync.

Which plan includes wholesale tools?

Wholesale and Scale. Starter does not include them.
