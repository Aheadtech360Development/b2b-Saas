## Prompt

yar complex ha smjh nh arha ha ye mjhy thora sai se misal deke smjhao yar mix up horha ha matlab iski need kia ha

## Response snapshot

Plain story with no technical words: someone wants to gift clothes for Christmas but does not know the friend's size, buys a $50 gift card, the friend gets a code by email, picks a $70 hoodie in the right size, $50 comes off with the code and $20 is paid by card (or $20 stays on the card if the hoodie costs $30). The need: the shop gets the money first, a new customer arrives, no wrong-size gifts, an upset customer can get a gift card instead of a refund so the money stays in the shop, and retail shops expect it (Shopify has it; the manager's Retail plan asked for it). The 30 minute hold and the rest are only guards against mistakes and cheating.
