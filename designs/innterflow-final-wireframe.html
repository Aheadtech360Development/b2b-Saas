Questions about DTF & UV DTF

What's the difference between Standard and UV DTF?

Standard DTF is heat-pressed onto fabric. UV DTF is peel-and-stick, no heat press needed, and works on hard surfaces too.

Which product should I order?

Multiple designs to fit together, build a gang sheet. One print-ready file, upload it. One design and one size, order by size.
